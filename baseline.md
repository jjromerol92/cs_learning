# P0 Baseline — Official-Syllabus Placement

This file is the answer to “where is the baseline and what do I put there?”

The baseline is **not a custom quiz**. It is a record of your attempts on public
work from Berkeley CS 61A, CS 61B, CS 70, CS 61C and CS 162.

## Rules

1. Attempt the official item before opening a posted solution.
2. Do not ask an AI agent to solve an official question.
3. An agent may clarify terminology, ask concept questions, or review your
   completed reasoning.
4. When an autograder/starter repository is unavailable, mark `ACCESS-BLOCKED`.
   Do not replace it with an invented task and do not score it as wrong.
5. Record raw evidence. Confidence alone does not count.

## Status vocabulary

- `NOT-STARTED`
- `ATTEMPTED`
- `COMPLETED`
- `ACCESS-BLOCKED`
- `REVISIT`

## Per-item scoring

For manually checkable questions:

- **2 — Independent:** correct before viewing a solution; can explain why.
- **1 — Assisted/corrected:** incomplete or incorrect initially, then corrected
  after a hint or official solution; can now explain.
- **0 — Unresolved:** still cannot explain the reasoning.
- **N/A — Access-blocked:** the material itself could not be accessed.

Do not turn course points into a fake universal percentage. Record both the
course's own result (where available) and the 0/1/2 learning score.

### Decision rules

- **NORMAL:** complete the listed week and official core work.
- **REMEDIATION:** add prerequisite reading and repeat unresolved official work.
- **COMPRESSED-REVIEW:** allowed only for a *specific week*, when the relevant
  official work is independently correct and the oral explanation scores 2.
  You still read that week's manifest and complete its retrieval check.
- `ACCESS-BLOCKED` is not evidence of mastery and not evidence of weakness.
