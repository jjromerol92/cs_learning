# Source Syllabus Inventory

This is the master map from our 60-week sequence to the selected universities'
actual public syllabi and assignments.

## Labels

- **OFFICIAL:** appears in the linked university course/textbook.
- **SYNTHESIZED TARGET:** our statement of the competence expected after the
  official unit. It is not an assignment.
- **SUPPLEMENTAL:** an optional agent-generated exercise. None is required by
  default.
- **ACCESS-BLOCKED:** official material or infrastructure cannot be reached.
  It must not be silently replaced.
