What changed

Foundation first

The old plan entered operating systems too quickly. This version adds programming abstraction, proof, algorithms, data structures and machine structures before CS 162.

Free required path

Every required lecture, text, specification, paper or documentation source is openly accessible. Paid books are optional only.

Built for your profile

Examples and projects use threat intelligence, telemetry, Linux, networking and data pipelines. General physics and calculus are not repeated unless diagnostics show a gap.

Evidence gates

You advance on deliverables, tests, explanations and a 10/12 rubric—not on passive video completion.

Non-negotiable decomposition ladder

  1. What observable behavior is required?
  2. What enters and leaves?
  3. What state exists and who owns it?
  4. What must always remain true?
  5. What transitions or transformations exist?
  6. Which interfaces separate them?
  7. What can fail before, during and after each transition?
  8. How is each component tested alone?
  9. What workload dominates cost?
  10. Which assumptions require measurement?
Advancement: score specification, decomposition, correctness, testing, systems/algorithm reasoning and explanation from 0–2. Pass is 10/12 with no zero.
